App: Open Season: Dates & Draws (com.osdatesanddraws.app)
Issue cited: Misleading Claims - Broken or Inaccessible Source Link

We are contesting this finding as factually incorrect. We are NOT claiming
government affiliation; we are an independent app and say so in our
description.

WHAT THE DESCRIPTION CONTAINS

Our full description already does everything the policy's "How to fix"
section asks for:

1. A prominent disclaimer in the second paragraph, in capitals:
   "DISCLAIMER: Open Season is an independent app by Piece & Quiet, LLC.
   It is NOT a government app and does not represent any government
   entity."

2. Every source of government information, enumerated by state, with the
   official website for each - all 50 state wildlife agencies plus
   Recreation.gov for federal permit hunts, and a statement that no other
   sources are used.

3. A single published index of every exact source URL we read, at
   https://osdatesanddraws.com/sources.html (publicly accessible, returns
   HTTP 200 to any client).

4. Every individual date inside the app links to the official source page
   it came from.

WHY THE "BROKEN LINK" FINDING IS INCORRECT

We tested every domain listed in the description. All of them load
normally in a web browser. However, ten of the state agency websites
return HTTP 403 (Forbidden) or drop the connection when requested by an
automated client rather than a browser, because those state government
sites use bot-protection services:

   - azgfd.com (Arizona Game & Fish Department)
   - dec.ny.gov (New York State Dept. of Environmental Conservation)
   - dlnr.hawaii.gov (Hawaii DLNR)
   - in.gov (Indiana DNR)
   - ksoutdoors.gov (Kansas Dept. of Wildlife & Parks)
   - mass.gov (Massachusetts Div. of Fisheries & Wildlife)
   - ndow.org (Nevada Dept. of Wildlife)
   - outdoornebraska.gov (Nebraska Game and Parks Commission)
   - tn.gov (Tennessee Wildlife Resources Agency)
   - wildlife.nh.gov (New Hampshire Fish and Game Department)

These are the genuine, official websites of those state wildlife
agencies. They are publicly accessible to any person using a browser.
They cannot be replaced with different links, because they are the
authoritative government sources for the hunting season dates our app
reports - which is precisely what this policy requires us to cite.

THE RESULT IS A REQUIREMENT WE CANNOT SATISFY

Across previous reviews of this listing we have been told, in turn, that
naming the sources without URLs is "Insufficient Sources Provided," and
that providing the URLs is a "Broken or Inaccessible Source Link." The
links are not broken. Any reviewer can confirm this in seconds by
opening, for example, https://www.azgfd.com or https://dec.ny.gov in a
browser.

WHAT WE ARE ASKING

We ask that a reviewer verify these source links in a browser rather than
with an automated checker, and approve the listing. We are glad to make
any concrete change that would resolve this - if there is a specific link
you need removed or reworded, please name it and we will change it the
same day.

Thank you for your time.
