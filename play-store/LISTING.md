## Full description  (max 4000 chars)
```
Never miss an opener or a tag deadline again.

Open Season tracks hunting season dates and draw/application deadlines for every state you hunt — and reminds you before every date that matters. No more digging through PDF regulations or missing a draw by a day.

PICK WHAT YOU HUNT
Choose your states and the species you chase. Open Season shows only what's legal where you hunt — deer, elk, bear, turkey, waterfowl, upland, and more.

EVERY SEASON, BY METHOD
Archery, muzzleloader, firearm, general — see every open window for every species you follow, sorted soonest-first with a live countdown to opening day.

TAG & DRAW DEADLINES
Application windows with countdowns, so you're never late on a draw. Get a reminder for the deadline — and for when results are expected.

REMINDERS THAT MATTER
A push notification before every opener and every deadline. Choose how far ahead — from a year out down to the day of.

REAL DATES, OFFICIAL SOURCES
Every date links back to the official state wildlife agency, with a "last verified" stamp. See something off? Report it in one tap.

BUILT FOR HUNTERS
- All 50 states
- Add every state and species you hunt
- Resident vs. nonresident context
- Add openers and deadlines straight to your calendar
- Buy-a-license and apply links to the official portals

Open Season is informational only. Season dates and regulations may contain errors or become outdated — always confirm with the official state wildlife agency before hunting or applying for a tag.
```
