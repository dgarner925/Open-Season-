Never miss an opener again. Open Season: Dates & Draws tracks hunting season dates, tag and draw application deadlines, and federal permit hunts across all 50 states.

DISCLAIMER: Open Season is an independent app by Piece & Quiet, LLC. It is NOT a government app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government agency. Always confirm dates with the official regulations, linked from every date in the app, before you hunt.

PICK YOUR HUNTS
Follow the states and species you hunt. Your home screen shows what is open now and what is opening next.

SEASON DATES, STATE BY STATE
Every season carries its dates and method (archery, muzzleloader, rifle) plus a direct link to its official state agency source page.

DRAW & TAG DEADLINES
Open Season tracks application windows so you apply on time, this year and every year.

FEDERAL PERMIT HUNTS
Managed hunts on national wildlife refuges, national forests, and Corps of Engineers lands, sourced only from Recreation.gov, with direct links to apply there.

REMINDERS THAT WORK (Pro)
Reminders per season and calendar export. Free: browse everything, all 50 states. Pro ($9.99/year): unlimited follows, reminders, calendar export.
