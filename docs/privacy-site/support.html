Common questions

How do I add states and species?

Tap Manage your hunts on the Home screen (or re-run the welcome flow) to add or remove any state and the species you hunt. Home, Seasons, and Tags update to match.

Why don't I see dates for a species I follow?

Season and deadline data is compiled from official state wildlife-agency documents and is added state-by-state. If a species shows "No dates yet," we either haven't published that state's current-season dates yet or the agency hasn't released them. It will appear once it's verified.

How do reminders work?

Enable notifications during setup (or in your device Settings). Open Season sends a push reminder before each season opener and application deadline for what you follow, so you never miss a date.

Do you store my licensing-portal passwords?

Never. The application tracker captures a username and link only — keep the password in your phone's password manager. We never ask for portal passwords.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Delete account. Deletion is immediate and permanent, and removes your profile, follows, alert settings, and tracked applications.

Important disclaimer

Season dates and regulations shown in Open Season are informational only and may contain errors or become outdated. Always confirm dates and rules with the official state wildlife agency before hunting or applying for a tag.
