Delete Your Account

Open Season: Dates & Draws — Piece & Quiet, LLC

You can permanently delete your Open Season: Dates & Draws account and its associated data at any time, either directly in the app or by emailing us.

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the app and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Open Account and choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Delete by email

If you can no longer access the app, email contact@osdatesanddraws.com from the address on your account with the subject line “Delete my account.” We will verify the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in credentials (email address, or Apple/Google sign-in link).
  • Your followed states and species, alert preferences, and reminder settings.
  • Your device push-notification tokens.
  • Any application-tracker entries you created.

What may be kept

  • Subscription records: purchase and billing history is processed by Apple or Google and our subscription processor (RevenueCat) and may be retained by them as required for accounting and fraud prevention. Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription — manage that in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Legal records: minimal records we are required to keep by law, for no longer than the law requires.

Deletion is permanent. Your follows, reminders, and tracker entries cannot be recovered after the account is deleted.
