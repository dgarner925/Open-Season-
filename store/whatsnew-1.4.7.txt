Leftover tags, and the hour they go on sale.

When a state puts its unclaimed tags back on the shelf, the good units are gone the same morning. Oregon opens at 10 a.m. Pacific on the first of July, and nobody announces it twice.

So it has a screen of its own now, on the Tags tab. What each state actually does — first come first served, a sign-up window, or sold until they run out — when it happens, and what tends to catch people out. Every date carries its hour and its time zone, because 10:00 AM in Colorado is not 10:00 AM in Georgia.

Follow a state and a species, and Open Season Pro tells you a week before, the day before, and on the morning itself, hours ahead of the sale rather than after it.

Also: the species page lost its medallion, and the mark is back in the top-left corner where it belongs.
